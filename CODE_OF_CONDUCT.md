# Code of Conduct (Contributor Covenant v2.1 + AI Clause)

## Our Pledge
We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation. We also welcome contributions created with the assistance of AI tools, provided a human contributor reviews, tests, and accepts responsibility for the result.

## Our Standards
Positive examples include:
- Using welcoming, inclusive language and being respectful of differing viewpoints.
- Offering constructive feedback and accepting it gracefully.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the project.
- Clearly labelling and reviewing any AI-assisted code or content.

Unacceptable behaviour includes:
- Harassment, trolling, or derogatory comments.
- Publishing others’ private information without explicit permission.
- Submitting unchecked AI-generated code/content as if it were reviewed.
- Any conduct that violates applicable laws or licences.

## Responsibilities
Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying behaviour standards and may remove, edit, or reject contributions that do not align with this Code.

## Scope
This Code applies within project spaces and in public spaces when an individual is representing the project.

## Enforcement
Report issues via GitHub Issues or by email: **[maintainers@example.com]** (replace with a real contact). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

## Enforcement Guidelines
1. **Correction** – Private, written warning with clarity on expected behaviour.
2. **Warning** – Official warning; continued violations may lead to temporary ban.
3. **Temporary Ban** – A time-limited restriction from interaction.
4. **Permanent Ban** – Removal from the community for severe/repeated violations.
